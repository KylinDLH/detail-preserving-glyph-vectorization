THE PRACTICAL PROBLEM

Scarce material.
Uneven image quality.
A need for faithful outlines.

The project is motivated by the limited availability and uneven quality of the real bamboo- and wooden-slip images available for development. Fine marks become difficult to separate from background interference, yet aggressive cleanup can remove the very details that make a glyph useful.

Clean glyph–SVG pairs are also limited. Controlled synthesis provides a tractable way to study that trade-off. The archived pipeline models shape variation, edge wear, internal ink loss and local deletion, then composites the damaged foreground with background crops. By retaining the source glyph and recording the imposed degradation, it supplies output-independent references for measuring both missing ink and residual background.

The present quantitative study tests new degradation instances within the trained font and glyph domain. It does not establish unrestricted transfer to historical manuscripts.

Evaluation scope and unavailable comparisons

Base glyphs and fonts are training-covered; background sources may overlap with earlier material. The test concerns newly generated degradations, not unseen glyph identities or unrestricted real-manuscript generalization. S1 is domain-trained; DocRes is a generic public model. Additional local diagnostics are exploratory.

Official OmniSVG, StarVector and LIVE were not run on this main test (0/100 each). Earlier stopped or blocked runs are not counted as full-test failures. All 100 registered inputs remain in the reported comparison.

These measurements are reference-relative geometric quantities, not expert historical annotations or a certification of review-free usability.

A REUSABLE REPRESENTATION

One extraction.
Several ways to use it.

High-quality extraction is the central capability. The following are intended applications, not additional benchmark claims.

01 / Collect & study

Character collections

Organize source-specific glyph forms and inspect contour differences between variants without keeping the surrounding background.

02 / Display & publish

Scalable visual material

Reuse outlines in digital exhibitions, research illustrations and publication layouts while retaining access to the original extraction.

03 / Edit & reuse

Editable vector assets

Adjust vector paths for later design or research workflows. Optional regularization remains separate from the source-preserving result.
